Before we talk.

A few useful details for your first conversation.

What should we share for a retrofit review?

Your server or appliance specifications, GPU model and memory, system RAM, storage and network setup, plus the models and workloads you want to run. We use this to assess suitability and identify any upgrades before preparing a quote.

Can you help connect our applications?

Connect your favourite apps and harnesses through our documented inference API. For custom workflows, we can scope a separate integration engagement with a measurable outcome.

Where will the system run?

At your premises or in a data centre you contract and control. Inference runs inside your environment, on a new appliance or suitable existing hardware.

How is Product source licensed?

Original first-party LLM Machines Product source is source-available under the unmodified PolyForm Internal Use License 1.0.0. Third-party software keeps its upstream licence.
