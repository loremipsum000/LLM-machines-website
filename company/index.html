04 — Operating principle

We don't see your data. By design.

The most important sentence in our remote management posture. It's not a promise — it's a structural property of the product.

What we monitor

Hardware and software only.

GPU temperatures, queue depths, model error rates, software-update status. Server fans, NVMe health, network throughput. Audit log volumes (sizes, not contents). Self-test results. License daemon heartbeats. That's it.

What we never see

The data plane is air-gapped from us.

User prompts. Retrieved documents. Customer data of any kind. Inference inputs or outputs. PII before or after redaction. Workflow payloads. We don't see them — not because we promise not to, but because the product is built so we can't.

Configurable posture

Decline BMC access — no retainer change.

Default is full BMC access (so we can monitor hardware health, push firmware, restart nodes). If you decline, we still deliver software updates and incident response, just without hardware-level telemetry. The retainer price is the same either way — the operational posture is your decision, not ours.

05 — Sovereignty

Built in the EU.
Built for the EU.

Sovereignty isn't a feature — it's the foundation. Every default, every component, every deployment is engineered to satisfy the four pillars of European digital regulation, not retrofitted to scrape past them.

EU AI ACT — Compliance by deployment

Audit trails, transparency, human oversight.

High-risk AI systems must be inspectable, documented, and supervised. On-premise deployment makes every prompt, response, and decision auditable inside your perimeter — not hidden behind a third-party API. The EU AI Act fully applies from August 2026; we are already ready.

GDPR — Data minimisation by design

Personal data never leaves your network.

Trade secrets, customer PII, and confidential records stay where they belong. Microsoft Presidio detects and redacts sensitive data before it ever reaches a model — and nothing crosses your firewall to begin with. Data subject access requests stay tractable because everything's in your environment.

NIS2 — Supply-chain risk, eliminated

Remove the cloud-AI link from your security perimeter.

NIS2 expanded cybersecurity obligations to essential and important entities and put supply-chain risk on the board agenda. With LLM Machines, the cloud-AI dependency simply isn't there to manage — one fewer foreign vendor in your risk register, one fewer relationship to vet annually.

EU DATA ACT — Portability, by default

Your stack. Your rights. No lock-in.

The Data Act explicitly targets cloud lock-in and mandates portability and switching rights. Our open-source foundation and one-time license honour that principle by default — your stack is yours to keep, swap, or extend. No "switching tax." No surprise migration cost. No proprietary format you can't take elsewhere.

06 — Beyond residency

Sovereignty is more than where data sits.

For AI workloads, sovereignty depends on who controls the data plane, the operating model and the technology stack.

Data sovereignty

Your prompts, documents and logs stay local.

The appliance runs inside your data centre, private cloud or dedicated Croatian hosting environment. Sensitive data does not need to cross into a third-party AI API.

Operational sovereignty

You decide who can access the system.

Identity, roles, support access and BMC access remain under your control. You can decline remote infrastructure access without changing the retainer.

Technology sovereignty

Open components keep switching possible.

Open-source foundations, model portability and an OpenAI-compatible gateway reduce dependency on one provider, one model family or one proprietary control plane.
