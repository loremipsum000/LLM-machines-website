04 — Operating principle

Nothing leaves your infrastructure.

The most important sentence in our operating model. It defines the boundary for every customer-premises deployment.

Local observability

Operational metadata only.

The appliance records infrastructure health, model availability, capacity, alerts, update state and metadata-only activity and audit events inside the customer environment. Workload content is not retained by LLM Machines-managed components.

Default data boundary

The data plane is isolated from LLM Machines by default.

User prompts, inference inputs and outputs, Application traffic and agentic workflow payloads remain inside the customer infrastructure.

Remote support posture

Remote access is off by default.

Support access is enabled only with explicit customer approval, for a defined time window and with an audit record. Local metrics and alerts remain available to your Operators without opening a standing remote path.

05 — Sovereignty

Built in the EU.
Built for the EU.

Sovereignty isn't a feature — it's the foundation. Every default, every component, every deployment is designed to support regulated European operation, with deployment-specific evidence and review still required.

EU AI ACT — Auditability by deployment

Audit trails, transparency, human oversight.

High-risk AI systems must be inspectable, documented and supervised. Metadata-only activity records, signed audit exports and customer-controlled identity provide a strong operational foundation for use-case-specific documentation and oversight.

GDPR — Data minimisation by design

Personal data stays local by default.

Trade secrets, personal data and confidential records remain inside your infrastructure. LLM Machines-managed components do not retain prompts, responses or request and response bodies.

NIS2 — Cloud-AI supplier risk, reduced

Remove the cloud-AI link from your security perimeter.

NIS2 expanded cybersecurity obligations to essential and important entities and put supply-chain risk on the board agenda. Customer-premises inference reduces dependency on external AI APIs while preserving local identity, observability and operational control.

EU DATA ACT — Portability, by default

Your stack. Your rights. Designed for portability.

The Data Act targets cloud lock-in and reinforces portability and switching rights. Open components, portable models and a documented API contract keep the appliance inspectable and the application boundary familiar.

06 — Beyond residency

Sovereignty is more than where data sits.

For AI workloads, sovereignty depends on who controls the data plane, the operating model and the technology stack.

Data sovereignty

Your inference stays local.

The appliance runs inside your infrastructure. Prompts, responses and workload content remain within the environment you control.

Operational sovereignty

You decide who can access the system.

Identity, Admin and Operator roles, support access and infrastructure controls remain under your authority. Remote support is off by default and explicitly enabled when needed.

Technology sovereignty

Open components keep switching possible.

Open-source foundations, model portability and the documented Models and Chat Completions endpoints reduce dependency on one provider, one model family or one proprietary control plane.
