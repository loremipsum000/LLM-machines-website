Sovereign AI

Sovereign AI for European Enterprises

Sovereign AI is direct control over the physical environment, model selection, update windows, operational access and the applications that use your private inference API.

01 · Definition

What sovereign AI has to control.

For regulated European enterprises, sovereignty has to cover infrastructure, model policy, lifecycle operations and the application boundary.

Physical control

Nothing leaves your infrastructure.

Inference runs on infrastructure at your site or in a data centre you contract and control, keeping workload content inside the environment you govern.

Model control

You approve what runs.

Your organisation selects approved models, revisions, routes and access policies while upstream-licensed third-party components preserve model choice and portability.

Operational control

Updates and access follow your policy.

Identity, support access, maintenance windows, update approval, metadata-only audit, backup and recovery operate within your authority.

02 · Regulation

Why this matters in Europe.

EU AI Act, GDPR, NIS2 and the EU Data Act all push enterprises toward clearer control over AI processing, auditability and portability.

Compliance is easier when the system is inspectable.

Local inference, customer-controlled identity and signed metadata-only audit exports provide evidence without retaining workload content.

  • Metadata-only activity and audit records
  • Human oversight with Admin and Operator roles
  • Local observability and operational controls

Your applications stay yours.

Chat Completions is the documented API protocol your favourite apps and harnesses use to access approved local models. Your organisation controls the application experience, permissions and workflow. Optional integration consulting is separately scoped and handed back into customer ownership.

  • Customer-owned applications
  • Separately scoped consulting
  • Local model access policies
