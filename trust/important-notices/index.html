The complete Product is not offered as a single open-source work. The approved licensing model for original first-party LLM Machines Product source is the unmodified PolyForm Internal Use License 1.0.0, making that original first-party source available for the licence's permitted internal use.

The delivered licence permits Customer internal business use and internal changes and new works based on the delivered First-Party Product Source. It does not permit distribution, sublicensing, or transfer of that licence to another party. The exact licence text delivered with the Product controls.

2. Third-party software and model material

Third-party applications, libraries, images, vendored source, upstream-derived source, and other upstream material remain governed by their existing upstream licences. The PolyForm licence does not replace, narrow, or relabel an upstream licence or its notice, source, and redistribution obligations.

Model weights and model licences remain separate from both First-Party Product Source and third-party software licences. The applicable release materials identify the delivered component, licence, notice, and SBOM boundaries.

3. Maintenance is separate from delivered rights

Maintenance entitlement governs paid access to future qualified releases, recovery retrieval, and support as stated in the signed agreement. An ACTIVE, GRACE, or FROZEN maintenance state does not stop an existing Appliance, invalidate an already delivered source licence, or expire an exported recovery capsule.

Customer modifications may fall outside the supported configuration. The signed MSA, Order Form, licence schedule, and release documentation must state the support, warranty, update, and recovery consequences without narrowing the delivered source licence.

5. Product and customer-application boundary

The standard Product boundary covers the Product edge, managed private inference, documented Models and Chat Completions APIs, identity and credentials, operational Console, observability, lifecycle, backup, recovery, and supported controls described for the applicable qualified release.

Customer-selected applications, their interfaces, data, permissions, tool effects, and workflows remain Customer-owned. Optional application or workflow integration requires a separate Statement of Work with measurable acceptance and documented post-handoff ownership.
