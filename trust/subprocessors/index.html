Third Parties and Subprocessors

Last updated: 3 August 2026 Status: Draft pending counsel and management approval. Not approved for publication or customer reliance.

This register separates (1) subprocessors used when LLM Machines processes Customer personal data on documented instructions, (2) providers used for LLM Machines' own website and business operations, and (3) suppliers that do not process personal data for us by default.

This register is not a substitute for a signed Data Processing Agreement. Customer-specific subprocessors, transfer safeguards, notice periods, objection procedures, and contractual rights are governed by the applicable signed DPA and Order Form.

Customer-data subprocessors

Current approved list: none.

Nothing leaves your infrastructure. For workloads processed within the supported Core Appliance boundary, prompts, model responses, tool arguments, tool results, request and response bodies, operational metadata, and audit records remain inside the Customer-controlled deployment. LLM Machines operates the managed inference core locally rather than as a hosted model-inference service.

Before any third party processes Customer personal data or accesses a Customer environment on LLM Machines' behalf, that provider must complete the applicable security, privacy, contractual, transfer, and management review and must be added to this list.

Hardware suppliers

These suppliers support hardware procurement or manufacturing. They are not Customer-data subprocessors by default. Their classification must change before any authorised support, RMA, diagnostics, logistics, or media-handling workflow gives them Customer personal data or Customer-environment access.

Supplier Function Default access Draft status
Distributor-backed procurement channel Infrastructure procurement channel selected for each Order Form to supply the sized Supermicro hardware. The exact contracting distributor and applicable warranty terms are confirmed in the supplier documents and signed Order Form. No Customer personal data or Customer-environment access by default. Supplier, not subprocessor. Identification, contracting, RMA, diagnostics, media-handling, and management approval are required for the applicable order.

No Customer personal data or Customer-environment access by default. Supplier/manufacturer, not subprocessor. Warranty, RMA, diagnostics, media-handling, and management approval pending.

Change notice

LLM Machines will provide written notice before adding or replacing a third party that processes Customer personal data on its behalf, in accordance with the applicable signed DPA. The notice period and objection procedure are those stated in that DPA; this page does not create a separate contractual right.

Material changes to website providers and hardware suppliers will be reflected here, but they are not Customer-data subprocessor changes unless their data-access role changes.

Questions can be sent to privacy@llm-machines.com. The final mailbox-provider and monitoring review must be completed before that address is relied on as the sole rights-request channel.
