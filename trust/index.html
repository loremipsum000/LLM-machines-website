Product and data boundary

Nothing leaves your infrastructure. For workloads processed within the supported Core Appliance boundary, prompts, model responses, tool arguments, tool results, request and response bodies, operational metadata, and audit records remain inside the Customer-controlled deployment. LLM Machines operates the managed inference core locally rather than as a hosted model-inference service.

LLM Machines provides managed local inference and a documented API contract. Chat Completions is the documented API protocol your favourite applications and harnesses use to access approved local models.

Customer-configured applications and explicitly authorised, time-limited support sessions operate under the boundaries and controls documented in the applicable signed agreement.

Product source, third-party software, and maintenance

The complete Product is not offered as a single open-source work. Original first-party LLM Machines Product source is source-available under the unmodified PolyForm Internal Use License 1.0.0. The delivered licence permits Customer internal business use and internal changes. It does not permit distribution, sublicensing, or transfer.

Third-party applications, libraries, images, vendored source, upstream-derived source, and other upstream material remain under their existing upstream licences. The PolyForm licence does not replace, narrow, or relabel those licences or their notice, source, and redistribution obligations.

Maintenance entitlement is separate from source licensing. It controls future qualified releases, recovery retrieval, and support as stated in the signed agreement. Expiry, grace, or freeze status does not stop an existing Appliance, invalidate an already delivered source licence, or expire an exported recovery capsule.

LLM Machines names, trademarks, logos, brand assets, and website editorial and creative content are outside the Product source licence unless expressly licensed. Copyright © 2026 LLM Machines. The "all rights reserved" statement applies only to that excluded material owned by LLM Machines, except where a separate licence or third-party right applies. It does not replace or narrow the Product or upstream software licences. The exact licensor and legal copyright holder remain publication blockers. See the draft Important Notices.

How we classify third parties

  • Customer-data subprocessors are third parties that process Customer personal data or access a Customer environment on LLM Machines' behalf.
  • Website and business operations providers support website hosting, functional assets, email routing, or internal business operations. Depending on the service, they may act as processors, separate controllers, or other service providers.
  • Hardware suppliers supply or manufacture Appliance hardware without Customer-data or Customer-environment access by default.

A supplier or business provider is reclassified before use if its role changes and it will process Customer personal data or access a Customer environment on LLM Machines' behalf.
