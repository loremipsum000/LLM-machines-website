Onboarding

Deploy On-Prem AI with a Clear Handoff

A tailored deployment, not a SaaS sign-up. We size the appliance, install local inference and the LXC agentic runtime, federate identity, connect your applications, validate workloads and hand over an operational system.

00 · Deployment path

From discovery to handoff.

Every deployment follows the same controlled path inside the customer infrastructure.

01

Discovery and sizing.

Map users, workloads, model needs, data sources, compliance constraints and expected capacity before hardware is finalised.

02

Hardware and first boot.

Rack, power, license, provision storage, validate GPUs, test network throughput and run local inference smoke tests.

03

Identity and roles.

Federate to your identity provider, map Admin and Operator access and confirm boundaries before rollout.

04

Applications and runtime.

Connect your favourite apps and harnesses, configure Application credentials and bring the LXC agentic runtime online.

05

Validation.

Test local inference, the documented API, agentic workflows, observability, metadata-only audit, backup and recovery with real users.

06

Training and handoff.

Train Admins and Operators, hand over runbooks and calendar the operational review.

01 · The operating model

One supported core. Your applications stay familiar.

The appliance combines local inference, an LXC runtime for agentic workflows and a documented contract for the applications your teams already use.

Core Appliance

Local inference and operational control.

Model serving, inference gateway, Console, identity, observability layer, metadata-only audit, offline updates, backup and recovery.

LXC agentic runtime

A dedicated runtime for agentic workflows.

An isolated LXC execution boundary with approved model access, governed credentials, resource policies and operational health checks.

Connected applications

Connect your favourite apps and harnesses.

Application credentials, model allowlists and the documented API contract connect existing client experiences to local inference.

Emergency Isolation

A controlled Admin action pauses Application inference and LXC agentic workflows while keeping the Console, identity, audit, hardware monitoring, backup and recovery available.

02 · The deployment sequence

Nine phases. Each one tailored to your environment.

Every phase has explicit owners, exit criteria and a clear statement of what is tailored to your environment.

00 01 02 03 04 05 06 07 08
00 Before delivery

Pre-shipment.

Discovery questionnaire covering your IdP, network topology, target applications, user count, model preferences and compliance constraints. Network pre-flight, power and rack specification, hardware-bound entitlement preparation and written sign-off.

Tailored to: your IdP type, compliance regime, target integrations
01 Installation

Hardware delivery & rack.

Receiving inspection against the manifest, rack installation, redundant power and network uplinks. Out-of-band management is configured to the agreed access posture before firmware and hardware validation.

Tailored to: your data-centre layout, network VLANs
02 First boot

First boot & licensing.

First-boot configuration covers hostname, time zone, NTP and initial Admin credentials. A signed offline entitlement is installed, storage is provisioned and self-tests validate GPUs, storage, network throughput and local inference.

Tailored to: your security and network posture
03 Platform configuration

Network & identity federation.

TLS certificates and DNS are configured for the approved local endpoints. Keycloak is federated to your IdP through OIDC or SAML, provisioning is verified and Admin and Operator role mappings are tested.

Tailored to: your identity provider and access model
04 Core services

Inference, Console and agentic runtime.

Approved models are loaded and registered with the inference gateway. Model aliases, Application credentials, allowlists and metadata-only audit are configured. The Console, observability layer and LXC agentic runtime are brought online as one operational system.

Tailored to: your approved models and capacity policy
05 Application connection

Connect your applications.

Connect your favourite apps and harnesses through Application credentials, explicit model allowlists and the documented Models and Chat Completions endpoints. Streaming and tool-call message transport are validated with the real client.

Tailored to: your existing applications and workflows
06 Acceptance

Validation suite.

End-to-end tests cover local inference, the documented API, connected applications, the LXC agentic runtime, observability, metadata-only audit, Emergency Isolation Mode, backup and recovery. Real users run a reproducible acceptance set.

Real users and reproducible acceptance criteria
07 Operational handoff

Training & handoff.

Admin and Operator training covers identity, Applications, model policy, Emergency Isolation Mode, signed audit export, offline updates, backup and recovery. A client-specific runbook, support channel and escalation path complete the handoff.

Tailored to: your team's roles, your support workflow
08 Operational review

Operational review.

Review Application usage metadata, model health, credential age, alerts, capacity and open operational actions. Confirm the runbook, update path and recovery posture against what the team learned after handoff.

Tailored to: your operational review cadence

04 · FAQ

Deployment questions.

What security, IT and business teams usually need to know before scheduling an on-prem AI rollout.

What do we need before deployment starts?

You need network details, identity provider access, Admin and Operator contacts, approved models, target applications and representative workloads for validation.

Can onboarding work for isolated environments?

Yes. Signed offline entitlements, local model bundles and signed manual update bundles support deployments without a public-internet dependency.

Which identity providers are supported?

The appliance supports standard enterprise identity federation through OIDC or SAML, with role mapping for Admin and Operator access.

How do applications connect?

Connect your favourite apps and harnesses through Application credentials, model allowlists and the documented Models and Chat Completions endpoints.

What makes a good pilot workload?

A good pilot has real users, clear success criteria, representative workload patterns and enough operational depth to validate the full deployment.
